Chapter 01

Foundations

What Bud is, who it's for, what it's not, and the two non-negotiables that shape every design choice.

The brand promise

Bud is an AI nutrition app for grocery shoppers managing food allergies. You scan any package, Bud reads the ingredients, and tells you whether it matches your profile. Another set of eyes.

Two non-negotiables

  1. People with allergies trust what we say. A false-negative verdict is the worst possible failure. Visual, verbal, and interaction design must never overpromise.
  2. Stay out of the Class II SaMD lane. Health Canada SOR/98-282 Rule 10(2). Saying "safe" reclassifies us as a regulated medical device and exposes the company to anaphylaxis tort liability. The verbal system is what keeps us legal.

What Bud is

  • An information tool. Not a medical device.
  • A second pair of eyes at the grocery aisle.
  • Built for the parent reading a label at 9pm with a toddler on one hip.
  • Built for the partner who's been told "trust me, it's fine."
  • Built for the adult newly diagnosed who suddenly has to read every label.

What Bud is NOT

  • Not a medical device.
  • Not a diagnostician.
  • Not a doctor / dietitian / RD.
  • Not a substitute for reading the physical label.
  • Not "safe", "guaranteed", "100% accurate", "FDA-approved", or "Health Canada approved".

The wedge

Bud reads the French side, the "may contain" line, the hidden derivatives, and Canadian private-label brands (PC, No Name, Compliments). No incumbent (Yuka, Fig, Spoonful, ContentChecked) does all four.

Brand soul

Co-founder mindset. Not an assistant.
Safety is sacred. False negatives are unacceptable. Flag uncertain results as "Uncertain", not "Compatible".
Trust is the currency. Verdicts cannot be bought, ever. No paid placement in alternatives.
Speed is a feature. An imperfect app in shoppers' hands beats a perfect prototype in Figma.

The promise to the user

"Another set of eyes."

Not safety. Not protection. Not prevention. We help you see what's printed and check it against your profile. The user still makes the decision.

Tagline (locked)

The Buddy You Always Needed.
