Chapter 02

Brand strategy

The wedge, the moat, the market, and what we deliberately won't compete on.

The wedge

Reads the label, not the database. Yuka, Fig, and Spoonful all rely on barcode lookups. They fail when the product isn't in their database (Canadian private label = 30 to 40% of groceries), when the manufacturer changes ingredients without updating, or when "may contain" warnings aren't captured.

Bud reads the physical package in real time. Multi-frame composition stitches text across curved or crinkled packaging. Every failed search becomes a data asset.

The moats

  1. Coverage. Private label + ingredient changes + "may contain" disclosures are uncatchable by barcode databases. We catch them by definition.
  2. Data flywheel. Every failed search fills our database. Two scans of the same product produce consensus. Coverage grows faster than Yuka's.
  3. Trust. Specific allergen call-outs build user trust faster than generic warnings. Trust compounds.
  4. Bilingual. Canadian product labels are mandatory bilingual. US apps don't handle this. We do natively.

The Canada First thesis

Canadian groceries are a data desert because:

  • 30 to 40% private label (Loblaw PC, Sobeys Compliments, Costco Kirkland)
  • Mandatory bilingual labeling breaks US-trained models
  • Smaller market = lower priority for incumbents

This means Bud has a moat from day 1 against Yuka and Fig in Canada. We own Canada. Then bilingual markets (Quebec, Belgium, Switzerland). Then US.

The market we own

Initial: 3 million Canadians with food allergies. 50% of Canadian households affected. $91.5B North American "Free-From" food market.
Phase 2: B2B Gap Analysis reports to small CPG brands. "5,000 Toronto shoppers put back your protein bar after seeing a gluten warning."
Phase 3: Bio-feedback + receipt + auto-reorder = end-to-end Health OS.

Boundaries (what we won't compete on)

  • Calorie tracking. Not our category.
  • Nutrition scoring (good/bad for you). Yuka owns this. Half-stepping it would dilute Bud.
  • Recipe / meal planning. Not our category.
  • Weight loss / body tracking. Not our category. Ever.

The funnel

  1. Acquire via TikTok + Instagram content + Allergen Card Kit Kickstarter June 30
  2. Activate with one scan that catches what they missed
  3. Retain via repeated trust events (every scan that's accurate)
  4. Monetize in Phase 2 with browser extension + premium insights
  5. Compound with B2B data revenue from the same data that's free for users
