Chapter 07

Iconography and imagery

One icon family. One mascot. Documentary photography. Zero stock.

Icon family (locked)

Lucide. One family for the entire app + marketing. Stroke 1.5px. No mixing with Material, Phosphor, Heroicons, Feather, or Ionicons.

Mascot anatomy (v0)

v6 LIVE

  • Body: round (perfect circle), sage-mint #bfd8c5 fill, 8% drop shadow
  • Eyes: two round, deep-sage #1d4433, 12% of body diameter
  • Eye spacing: ±18% of body diameter from center
  • Antenna: deep-sage #163826 stem, brand-500 glowing tip

What Bud never does

  • Talk (no speech bubbles in v1)
  • Have a mouth (v0 + v1 are mouth-less for design simplicity)
  • Hold props (no clipboard, flashlight, shopping basket)
  • Change color (always sage-mint body, deep-sage eyes)
  • Appear in marketing where the verdict is somber (Avoid screens get warning mood, never idle)

Photography direction

Aesthetic: Bon Appétit Healthyish meets Cereal Magazine. Documentary, naturalistic, restrained.

Rules

  • Real natural daylight, no studio softboxes, no harsh fluorescents
  • Warm oat + soft sage color palette in scene
  • Real Canadian private-label products visible (PC, No Name, Compliments). Never invented brands.
  • Real humans, not stock-photo models
  • No faces visible in most shots (hands, over-the-shoulder, POV, still life)
  • Asymmetric composition. Subject anchors one third, rest is negative space

Banned in photography

  • Glossy plastic-doll AI skin
  • Painterly / oil-paint look
  • Cartoon stylization
  • Symmetrical / centered subject
  • Studio softbox lighting
  • Stock-photo smile straight to camera
  • Golden-hour fantasy glow
  • Invented brand packaging
  • Bud branding on any packaging
