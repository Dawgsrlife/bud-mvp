Bud · a second opinion at the shelf

The brand book.

A working document for everyone who designs, writes, or codes anything that touches BUD. Restraint is the brand. This book holds the line.

The brand promise

BUD is the buddy at your shoulder that says "yes, you can eat this" or "no, put it back" before you even look at the label.

The five traits

  1. Quietly confident. Doesn't shout. Knows what it knows. Surfaces certainty when certain, doubt when honest.
  2. Hand-on-shoulder warmth. The voice of a friend who happens to have a chemistry PhD.
  3. Specific over generic. "Contains peanut, you flagged peanut" beats "may contain allergens."
  4. Tactile precision. Every interaction has weight. Springs settle. Buttons respond.
  5. Generous with the user, ruthless with itself. Free safety. Premium tier earns its money.

The verdict language

The verdict is the brand's load-bearing moment. Three states, all using the restrained card pattern (never a status-color screen flood).

COMPATIBLE UNCERTAIN AVOID

What's locked v4: Typography (Inter, 6-step scale, 3 weights). Motion (spring physics, no linear easing). Voice ladder + ban list (including em-dashes). Mascot mood map. Verdict screen pattern (restrained card). Color discipline (85% neutral).
What's in-flight: Brand color (4 ladder variants under test). Logo wordmark (current is text-only). Mascot face evolution (eyes-only v0). App icon direction. Photography style.
