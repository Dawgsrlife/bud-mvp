Chapter 04

Logo and identity

The wordmark, lockups, and what never to do with the Bud mark.

Wordmark

bud
bud
  • The wordmark is the word bud lowercase
  • Set in Sora 600
  • Deep sage #1d4433 on oat backgrounds, oat #fcf9f2 on sage backgrounds
  • Letter-spacing: -0.02em (tight)

Clear space

At least 1× the cap-height in all four directions around the wordmark. No other element may breach this zone.

Minimum sizes

  • Print: 8pt cap-height minimum. Below that, the wordmark loses optical weight.
  • Digital: 32px cap-height minimum.
  • Favicon / app icon: drop the wordmark, use the mascot mark only.

Logo system (5 lockups)

LockupUsed for
Primary wordmark onlyApp header, partner docs, web nav
Mascot mark onlyApp icon, social avatar, favicon
Horizontal: mascot + wordmarkEmail headers, signatures, business cards
Stacked: mascot above wordmarkVertical contexts, Instagram story header
Hero: stacked + tagline belowMarketing landing hero only

Never do this

  • Don't recolor outside brand-* or ink
  • Don't apply gradients
  • Don't apply drop shadows
  • Don't rotate
  • Don't crop the mascot (always full circle visible)
  • Don't replace the wordmark font with a substitute
  • Don't stretch
  • Don't put on busy photographic backgrounds — always solid surface or tint
  • Don't put Bud on a product (jar, box, bar, wrapper). Bud is the app, never a packaged product.
  • Don't invert the wordmark to pure white on dark — use #fcf9f2 oat (iOS auto-inverts pure white to muddy grey)
