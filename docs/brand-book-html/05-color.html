Chapter 05

Color

The Consumer System. Editorial palette that protects Bud from looking like every other AI-powered health app.

The discipline

85% of any screen is neutral. Oat background plus charcoal text. The eye rests on neutrals. Deep sage shows up only on CTAs, status pills, and brand moments. Terracotta shows up only for danger / Avoid states.

This is the most violated brand rule in fintech and health apps. Yuka, Fig, MyFitnessPal all flood color across the screen. Bud does not. Wealthsimple, Linear, Phantom Wallet all hold the line. Bud holds the line.

Status (the three verdicts)

Verdict pills are the only place status color appears. Never as page backgrounds. Never as full-bleed accents. Pills get 10% tint background + 25% border + dot.

Color usage rules

  1. One brand color per screen. Deep sage. Never two greens.
  2. Color on CTAs, status pills, brand moments. Never decorative.
  3. No gradients on buttons or backgrounds. Especially purple-to-pink, the number-one AI slop tell.
  4. No status-color flood. Yuka territory. Status colors stay in pills + tags + dots.
  5. One shadow per elevated element. No stacked shadows.
  6. Status pills use 10% tint + 25% border.
  7. Pure white and pure black are banned. Surface low and charcoal only.
