Typography

Inter Variable. Six sizes. Three weights. Discipline beats variety.

The family

Inter Variable for body and UI. Inter Display for hero numbers. Free, open-source, geometric, modern, wide language support (covers French for Canadian labeling).

Banned

  • Italic (use weight for emphasis)
  • Underline (except explicit links)
  • Mixing serif + sans (Inter only)
  • Font sizes between scale steps
  • Weight 300 or 900
  • All-caps body text
