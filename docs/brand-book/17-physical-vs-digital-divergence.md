# Chapter 17. Physical vs digital brand divergence

**Last touched:** 2026-05-15. Locked v4.
**Status:** Allowed divergence, documented.

## What happened

On May 15 2026, Amir was finalizing physical print materials (envelope + business-card-sized waitlist cards for in-person handout) at the same time Alex + Kasumi were locking the digital brand book.

The print direction Amir chose:
- **Forest deep green** (closer to v2-toronto-deep than the v1-emerald in the app)
- **Cream / warm off-white background** (closer to v4-wealthsimple-cream than the cool FAFAFA in the app)
- **Serif italic display** for hero ("The Buddy You Always Needed"), a Playfair / DM Serif style
- **Bud capitalized as "Bud"** (sentence case), NOT "BUD" all-caps eyebrow

The digital direction in the app + brand book:
- BUD green `#1FAB5C` (v1-emerald, the cooler safety green)
- Off-white `#FAFAFA`
- Inter Bold sans for hero
- "BUD" all-caps eyebrow + "Bud" character name lowercase

## The decision (locked 2026-05-15)

**Both stand.** Physical print uses Amir's direction. Digital app + web stays current. Documented divergence, not a violation.

Why: cards are going to print this week. Stopping Amir would lose physical-marketing momentum 6 weeks before the Kickstarter. The digital app is the load-bearing brand for the next 12 months; print is a one-off until next print run.

When we reconcile (Phase 2, post-Kickstarter):
- Likely swing the app's brand color toward Amir's deeper forest green (a v2-toronto-deep-shaped move)
- Likely keep Inter for body type but introduce a serif display for hero moments (Playfair Display or DM Serif Display)
- Likely soften the bg to warmer off-white (closer to cream)
- Verdict screens stay the locked restrained-card pattern regardless

## What this means right now

| Surface | Direction |
|---|---|
| **Print: waitlist cards** | Amir's forest + cream + serif italic (going to print) |
| **Print: envelopes** | Same as cards (natural texture / nature vibes) |
| **App: in-product UI** | Current brand book (v1-emerald + Inter + cool off-white) |
| **Web: bud.quest landing** | Lean toward Amir's direction since landing is brand-first, less utility-first. Update next iteration. |
| **Web: bud-brand-book.vercel.app** | Current digital direction. Documents the divergence. |
| **Social: Instagram + TikTok** | Lean toward Amir's direction (warmer / serif = better for content). Phase 2 social brand chapter. |

## Open question

**Does the in-app brand follow the print direction in Phase 2?** Decision deferred to post-MVP. The MVP ships in the current direction so we don't lose another week to a brand pivot. Post-launch we A/B the warmer/serif direction against the current cooler/sans direction in marketing copy + landing hero. Whichever wins on click-through rates wins the long-term brand.
