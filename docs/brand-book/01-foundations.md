# Chapter 01. Foundations

**Last touched:** 2026-05-15. Locked v4.

## The brand promise (one sentence)

**BUD is the buddy at your shoulder that says "yes, you can eat this" or "no, put it back" before you even look at the label.**

## What BUD is

BUD is an AI-powered mobile app that scans grocery products with the phone camera, reads the package text with on-device OCR, and gives shoppers with food allergies or dietary restrictions an instant Compatible / Avoid / Caution verdict against their personal allergen profile.

BUD is **a second opinion at the shelf, not a database lookup**. Where Yuka and Fig fail (private label, ingredient changes, "may contain" disclosures) BUD wins because it reads what's actually printed on the package.

## What BUD will become (the three phases)

| Phase | What | When |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Safety Check** | Free utility. Speed and safety. Gamified data via Bud Credits / Data Scout Protocol. Become the default tool before purchase. | MVP -> Kickstarter June 30 2026 |
| **2. Intelligence Layer** | Freemium ($5.99 / $8.99 / $60yr). Browser extension. Truth Reports (aggregated consumer feedback). B2B Gap Analysis for CPG brands. | Q4 2026 -> 2027 |
| **3. Bio-Feedback Loop** | Symptom-correlation diagnostic engine. Receipt digitization. Auto-reorder. Brands bid for placement on user reorders. | 2027+ |

## Who BUD is for

### Primary (MVP user)

People with **a clear list of things they cannot eat**, who currently:
- Spend 10+ minutes per grocery trip reading labels
- Have had a near-miss or a real reaction within the past year
- Don't trust the existing apps (Yuka skip the private label, Fig out of date)
- Are willing to scan a few products to find out if BUD is real

Concrete personas:
- **Parent of a peanut-allergic child.** High vigilance, low patience for false negatives. Will become a power user the moment BUD catches one "may contain" that Yuka missed.
- **Celiac adult.** Has been burned by hidden gluten. Wants confidence at the shelf.
- **Newly-diagnosed dairy intolerant.** Doesn't know the synonyms yet (whey, casein). Needs BUD to be the synonym expert.

### Secondary (Phase 2)

- **Vegan / vegetarian / halal / kosher / keto / low-FODMAP** users wanting fast confirmation against personal rules.
- **CPG product managers** wanting Gap Analysis reports on competing products' weak points.

### Not for (in MVP)

- People without a defined dietary restriction. They get no value from the verdict.
- People wanting a calorie counter or weight-loss app. Not our lane.
- People wanting to track "what is healthy in general." We're not a nutritionist.

## What BUD is NOT

- **Not a medical device.** No diagnoses. No prescriptions. Disclaimer present.
- **Not a tracking app.** No streaks. No "clean eating days." No gamified weight loss.
- **Not Instagram for food.** No social feed, no likes, no shares.
- **Not anxious.** We don't warn about every additive. We respect the user's profile.
- **Not surveillance.** We tell you what we know about the product, not what we know about you.
- **Not a database company first.** We are a verdict company first. The database is exhaust.

## The personality (5 traits, locked)

1. **Quietly confident.** Doesn't shout. Knows what it knows. Surfaces certainty when certain, doubt when honest. Never fakes.
2. **Hand-on-shoulder warmth.** The voice of a friend who happens to have a chemistry PhD. Concern is real, not performed.
3. **Specific over generic.** "Contains peanut, you flagged peanut" beats "may contain allergens."
4. **Tactile precision.** Every interaction has weight. Spring physics, haptic feedback, considered transitions.
5. **Generous with the user, ruthless with itself.** Free utility. No friction onboarding. Premium tier earns its money via genuinely better insight, never via withheld safety.
